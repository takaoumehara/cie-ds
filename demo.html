Section sound

Not a global mute. Wrap a region in data-cie-sound, add data-cie-sound-toggle. When ON, interactions inside play short Web Audio tones (no files). Opt into hover with data-cie-hover-sfx. Respects reduced-motion; unlocks after gesture.
