Section sound · Hover + Click

Not a global mute. Wrap a region in data-cie-sound, add data-cie-sound-toggle, enable data-cie-hover-sfx. When ON, interactions inside play short Web Audio tones (no files). Multiple presets available. This section plays sound on both hover AND click. Respects reduced-motion; unlocks after gesture.

Playable section

Speaker toggle · local only

Preset:

Toggle Sound ON first (you'll hear an immediate test beep), then choose a preset and click the controls above. The "Play Test" button (dark) lets you verify sound without changing state. Outside this panel, the rest of the page stays silent. Presets: soft-tick, paper-snap (default), glass-pip, low-thud, bright-confirm.

v0.4.1 fix: Sound now plays clearly (~0.4 gain), works with reduced-motion, robust unlock with retry.

Section sound · Click Only

No hover SFX — sound plays ONLY on click/activate, not on hover. Same preset system. Toggle ON and click controls—you'll hear clear tones without hover noise. Verify with "Play Test". Gain 0.4 (clearly audible).

Click-only section

No hover · Click/tap only

Preset:

Toggle Sound ON, then click the controls above. No sound on hover in this section (no data-cie-hover-sfx attribute). Perfect for users who find hover SFX distracting. Immediate test beep on toggle, loud "Play Test" confirms audio works.
